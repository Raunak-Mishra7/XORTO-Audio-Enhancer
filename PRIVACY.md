# XORTO Audio Enhancer Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 15, 2026

XORTO Audio Enhancer is a browser extension designed to enhance audio on YouTube through features such as volume boost, bass boost, stereo separation, reverb, and an optional audio visualizer.

## Data We Access

XORTO Audio Enhancer accesses the current YouTube media element only to provide its audio enhancement and visualization features.

This may include website content such as audio or video media that is currently playing on YouTube.

## How Data Is Used

All audio processing is performed locally in the user's browser.

XORTO Audio Enhancer does not upload, transmit, record, sell, or share YouTube audio, video content, browsing history, or personal information with the developer or any third party.

## Local Storage

XORTO Audio Enhancer uses Chrome's local storage API to save user preferences, including settings such as:

- Volume level
- Bass level
- Stereo separation
- Reverb settings
- Visualizer preferences
- Visualizer position

These settings remain stored locally in the user's browser.

## Third-Party Services

XORTO Audio Enhancer does not use analytics, advertising trackers, telemetry services, or remote servers for audio processing.

The extension includes optional donation links. These external pages are opened only when the user chooses to click a donation button. XORTO Audio Enhancer does not collect or process payment information.

## Data Sharing

XORTO Audio Enhancer does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

## Limited Use

The use of information handled by XORTO Audio Enhancer is limited solely to providing the extension's user-facing audio enhancement and visualization features.

XORTO Audio Enhancer complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
